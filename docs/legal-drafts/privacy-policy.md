## 1. What Reborna is

Reborna is an independent discovery and community platform for original reborn sculpture, artist credits, specialist materials and the people who make and collect them. The site currently provides editorial catalogue information, links to third-party sellers, member accounts, saved collections, private studio notes, private future-listing drafts, discussion forums, public member profiles, private messages and contact enquiries. Reborna **does not currently take payments or sell or fulfil products on behalf of sellers**. If these services change, this notice will need updating before launch.

This policy explains how personal information is used when you visit the site, register, take part in the community or contact us.

## 2. Information we handle

Depending on which features you use, Reborna may handle:

- **Account and security details:** email address, chosen public username and display name, password verification data (salt and hashed password, not your plain-text password), email verification status, login sessions, role and interest selections, communication preferences.
- **Public profile:** your username, display name, optional short biography, optional profile photograph, and self-selected roles. Your email address and private account preferences are not shown in a public profile.
- **Your private workspace:** items saved to a wishlist, items you mark as owned (this is your own description, not a verified purchase), private studio project notes, and draft listings. Draft listings are not public advertisements.
- **Community activity:** forum topics and replies, reactions, reports, moderation decisions and appeals. Messages between members are not published as forum content; they may require authorised safety or support review when reported or when necessary.
- **Enquiries and correspondence:** your name, email, enquiry category, message, and follow-up communications when you use the contact form or email us.
- **Technical/security data:** device/browser information, security challenge signals, network address handled by infrastructure providers, pseudonymous rate-limiting identifiers derived from network addresses, session cookies, site preferences, and error/security logs.

Please avoid sharing payment card information, passwords, medical information or other sensitive information in forum posts, messages or enquiry forms.

## 4. What other people can see

When you make your profile visible, visitors may see your public name, photo, biography and roles; they should not treat self-selected labels as verified professional credentials. Approved public posts and replies may be visible to visitors and indexed when Reborna opens to search engines.

Your personal wishlist, marked-owned items, studio notes and unpublished seller drafts are designed to be private to your account. Conversations are restricted to their participants, subject to limited, authorised safety and legal administration.

Do not post personal contact information you would not want seen publicly.

## 5. Automated content screening and human moderation

Reborna uses Cloudflare security checks to distinguish genuine visitors from automated abuse. The community also uses a content-safety model to assess submitted posts and replies. Content assessed as lower-risk may be published; content flagged as potentially unsafe, uncertain or affected by a model error may be held for human review. Automated screening is not a judgement of truth, authenticity or professional status.

People may criticise Reborna and third-party companies within the community guidelines. You can report harmful content and request a review of a moderation decision through hello@reborna.co. Safety screening of public posts is separate from any future automated processing of private messages.

## 6. Organisations involved in delivering Reborna

We use service providers including:

- **Cloudflare:** hosting, content delivery, bot prevention (Turnstile), application security, European-region community database storage, and certain AI-assisted community screening functions. Cloudflare's broader infrastructure can process information in other countries.
- **Resend:** sends registration verification, account recovery and other transactional email where configured.
- **Image hosts and external sources:** third-party artist, supplier or image-delivery providers may receive normal browser connection data when external catalogue images or outbound links load.

Only share information with providers for legitimate service purposes and on appropriate terms. No sale of member personal data is planned or authorised.

**Before publication:** verify processor agreements, exact provider lists, which third-party images/scripts load on each page, countries involved in data transfers and relevant transfer safeguards. Do not equate an EU-region database with an absolute promise that *all* processing remains in the EU.

## 7. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

The service uses an essential secure `rb_session` login cookie lasting up to 30 days, as well as anti-bot/security technologies when required. Certain optional-looking *features you actively request* use on-device storage—for example guest favourites (`reborna-wishlist-v1` and migration key `reborna-saved-original-kits`), the first-save invitation flag (`reborna-wishlist-first-save-invited-v1`) and your currency preference (`reborn-currency`). These are not designed for advertising tracking.

See our separate [Cookie & Storage Notice](/cookies/) for explanations, how to clear local preferences and any non-essential technologies discovered in the final audit. We will seek consent before using non-exempt tracking cookies or similar technologies, if introduced.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Your active account and its features require us to store associated data while you use Reborna. Verification/reset links expire (currently 24 hours and 30 minutes respectively), and authenticated session credentials expire after up to 30 days. The application also records limited moderation, anti-abuse and enquiry information.

**Mandatory retention review before publication:** define and actually implement a defensible schedule for unverified accounts, public posts, private messages, reports, backup copies, contact enquiries, security logs and stored newsletter choices. Automated account removal and self-service export are **not yet implemented**; do not promise those features. Verified people can ask us to provide, correct or erase their data using the email above, subject to legal exceptions, and we must be able to fulfil those requests operationally.

## 9. Your privacy rights and complaints

Depending on the circumstances and UK data-protection law, you can request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of or transfer of relevant personal data; object to some processing; and withdraw optional consent. We may need to verify your identity to avoid sharing somebody else's information.

Contact **hello@reborna.co** with the subject **Privacy request** or **Privacy complaint**. We will acknowledge data-protection complaints and follow the applicable statutory timetable. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk/.

**Operational requirement:** establish an actual complaints inbox workflow and track acknowledgements and replies; UK requirements effective in 2026 generally require acknowledgement of data-protection complaints within 30 days.
