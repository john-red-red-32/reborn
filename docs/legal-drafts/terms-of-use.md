## 1. Welcome to Reborna

Reborna (https://reborna.co) is an independent discovery and community website focused on original reborn sculptors, their authorised kits, finished reborn artistry, materials and the people who appreciate them. By browsing the site you accept reasonable website rules; registering an account requires you to accept these Terms and our [Community Guidelines](/community/guidelines/). See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for personal data handling.

These Terms describe **services that exist now**. Future facilities such as payments, public seller listings, commissions, transaction histories, seller verification and Studio Sessions booking require their own terms before launch.

## 2. Independent editorial content and authentic original works

We aim to distinguish the **original sculpt created by an artist**, a **kit manufactured under an original artist's authorisation**, and a **finished reborn doll created from such a kit**. Artist names, photographs, credits, availability, release labels and product references may change or contain errors. Older sculpt archives are not proof that any kit is presently manufactured or in stock.

Reborna is independent of artists, manufacturers and sellers unless clearly disclosed. Editorial inclusion is not an endorsement, guarantee of authenticity or certification of any retailer. You should confirm source, rights, price, condition, shipping and availability with the relevant retailer before buying.

We welcome corrections with supporting sources at hello@reborna.co.

## 3. Outside stores, purchases and orders

At present **Reborna does not process payments, hold customer funds, fulfil orders, operate escrow or offer buyer protection for purchases made through outside sellers**. Product links leave Reborna and any transaction is between you and the external merchant, subject to that merchant's terms and applicable consumer rights.

Saving an item, marking it “in my collection”, following a sculptor, starting a studio project or drafting a proposed seller listing is **not** a purchase, legal ownership verification, offer for sale or accepted order.

If Reborna later introduces its own marketplace, we will publish the applicable seller, buyer, payment, returns and complaints terms **before** transactions are enabled.

We may screen public discussions with automated moderation tools, publish lower-risk material, hold uncertain material for human review, and restrict, remove or suspend content/accounts where proportionate and necessary under these rules or the law. Automated tools can make mistakes. We will aim to explain substantive moderation decisions and provide a route to ask for reconsideration.
